  NODE 1: YD-ESP32-S3 v1.3 (MPU SENDER)

1. ARCHITECTURAL OVERVIEW:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The system uses two complementary Espressif SoC architectures linked over an ad-hoc,
low-latency (<5ms) ESP-NOW wireless link:

NODE 1: MPU SENDER (YD-ESP32-S3 v1.3)
- Chip Target: ESP32-S3 (Xtensa Dual-Core LX7)
- Sensors: GY-521 MPU-6050 6-Axis IMU connected via I2C0 (SDA GPIO 4, SCL GPIO 5)
- Firmware Image: build_out/sender/esp32s3_mpu_sender.bin
- Functions: Computes 6-axis complementary orientation fusion, runs stationary
  noise locking, multi-axis calibration, pairs automatically with the Arm
  Receiver via ESP-NOW discovery on Channel 1, hosts BLE NUS for the Web/iOS app,
  and transmits real-time motion vectors.
- Note: Avoid GPIO 18, 19, and 20 for I2C (reserved for native USB-JTAG).

NODE 2: ARM RECEIVER (Waveshare ESP32-C6-LCD-1.47)
- Chip Target: ESP32-C6 (RISC-V 160MHz)
- Onboard Display: 1.47" ST7789 IPS LCD (172x320) showing live 4-axis HUD
- Actuators: 3 MP90S/SG90 servos for 3-DOF Arm + 1 MG996R for Scissor Lift
- Signal Pins: Claw GPIO 0, Arm GPIO 1, Shoulder GPIO 2, Scissor Lift GPIO 3
- Firmware Image: build_out/receiver/esp32c6_arm_receiver.bin
- Functions: Receives wireless motion packets, drives 50Hz LEDC PWM channels,
  executes smooth sinusoidal easing, handles auto-idle detaching, and executes
  stored programmable actions.

2. HARDWARE WIRING SPECIFICATION:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. MPU SENDER BOARD (YD-ESP32-S3 v1.3):
   Pin   MPU-6050 Pin   YD-ESP32-S3 Pin   Function
   1     VCC            3.3V              3.3V Power
   2     GND            GND               Common Ground
   3     SCL            GPIO 5            I2C0 Clock (400kHz)
   4     SDA            GPIO 4            I2C0 Data  (400kHz)
   7     AD0            GND               I2C Address 0x68 (3.3V = 0x69)

B. ARM RECEIVER BOARD (Waveshare ESP32-C6-LCD-1.47):
   Servo Channel   Joint           ESP32-C6 Pin        Power Connection
   Common GND      Ground          GND                 Common Ground tied to external 5V PSU!
   Servo Power     5V Power        External 5V         5V 2.5A+ supply (Never power from 3.3V!)
   Servo 1 (S1)    Claw (Gripper)  GPIO 0              50Hz LEDC PWM (500-2500us)
   Servo 2 (S2)    Arm             GPIO 1              50Hz LEDC PWM (500-2500us)
   Servo 3 (S3)    Shoulder        GPIO 2              50Hz LEDC PWM (500-2500us)
   Servo 4 (S4)    Scissor Lift    GPIO 3              50Hz LEDC PWM (500-2500us)

3. AUTOMATIC ZERO-INTERVENTION ESP-NOW DISCOVERY & PAIRING:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Channel: Both boards operate on 2.4GHz Wi-Fi Channel 1 (WIFI_IF_STA).
2. Protocol Magic: 0x59444152 ("YDAR")
3. Discovery Cycle:
   - On boot, both boards initialize Wi-Fi in Station mode with coexistence enabled.
   - The Sender and Receiver broadcast periodic discovery beacons.
   - When a beacon from the complementary role is detected, unicast MAC addresses
     are registered and a two-way handshake completes in under 1 second.
   - If either board powers down, the link times out after 3.5s and automatically
     resumes beacon scanning until reconnected.
